My Annotation Approach

01 — Understand the guideline

I study the schema, definitions, examples, edge cases and decision rules before labeling.

02 — Observe before deciding

I base annotation decisions on observable evidence rather than assumptions, especially in visual data.

03 — Apply decisions consistently

I maintain consistent labeling decisions across samples and batches.

04 — Review for quality

I check for missed events, incorrect labels, boundary errors and inconsistencies before submission.
